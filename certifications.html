3 free IT certifications in 15 days

Before Day 1 (10 minutes): create free accounts on Microsoft Learn, the Fortinet Training Institute (FortiCloud "Email user" account) and Oracle MyLearn. Use the name on your passport, because it's printed on your certificates. Tick the boxes as you go. Your progress is saved in this browser.
